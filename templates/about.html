About the Brand

Our Story

Effortless Elegance, Reimagined

Founded by Safeera Abba, Dar Safeera represents a harmonious convergence of rich cultural heritage and contemporary modesty.

Operating out of the vibrant cities of Abuja and Lagos, Nigeria, Dar Safeera was established with a singular mission: to provide the modern Muslim woman with luxurious modest wear that is as comfortable as it is exquisitely elegant.

Every abaya we produce is the result of continuous design iteration, curated fabric sourcing, and detailed handcrafting. We produce our pieces in highly exclusive, limited editions, ensuring our clients receive a garment that stands out for its individuality and high fashion styling.

Exclusivity

No mass manufacturing. We focus on small collections that ensure absolute quality control and unique styling.

Premium Materials

From lightweight crêpe to premium silk blends, we select fabrics for their exquisite drape and breathability.
