# Dar Safeera – Product Image & Placeholder Guide

This website is styled with an ultra-premium editorial modish aesthetic. To maintain the luxury brand positioning, all real product images uploaded in the future must match this standard.

---

## Technical Specifications for Product Images

- **Aspect Ratio:** Strictly **3:4** (Vertical Portrait orientation). Recommended dimension: **1200px width by 1600px height**.
- **File Format:** JPEG or WebP (max quality 85–90% to balance speed with high detail).
- **Backgrounds:** Warm editorial, minimal studio backdrops, warm-white plaster walls, archways, soft beige limestone tiles, or neutral natural textures. Avoid busy urban backgrounds.
- **Lighting:** Soft natural daylight, warm golden hours, or diffused studio lighting. Avoid harsh shadows or high-contrast flashes.

## How to Upload Real Product Photos

1. Access the custom styled administration panel: [http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/](http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/)
2. Log in using your admin credentials.
3. Open **Catalog** → **Products** and select the product you wish to update.
4. Scroll to the **Product Images** inline editor at the bottom.
5. Upload your new images. Mark the primary portrait shot as **Is Primary**.
6. Save the product! The site will automatically transition from the elegant color-coordinated placeholder cards to your gorgeous real photographs instantly.
